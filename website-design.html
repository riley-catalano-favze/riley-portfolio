Website FAQ

Good questions.

Quick answers about how I design, build, and launch websites.

Ask me in a text 💬
What platforms do you build websites on?
I hand-code custom websites in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript hosted on GitHub, and I design websites on HubSpot. We pick the platform that fits how your team wants to update the site day to day.
Will my website show up on Google and in AI answers?
That is built in from the first page. Every site gets dedicated service pages, local pages for the areas you serve, structured data, and question-and-answer content written so Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini can understand and recommend your business.
Do you write the website copy too?
Yes. I write every page using the StoryBrand framework: your customer is the hero, your business is the guide, and each page leads to one clear next step.
How does a website fit into lead generation?
Your website is the front door of the whole system. Search visibility brings people in, the site earns their trust and captures the lead, and CRM and email automation follow up so every inquiry keeps moving toward a sale.
How do I get started?
